Who we are
Technocraft Associates

Public projects demand more than construction. They demand accountability.

Technocraft Associates is a Mumbai-based construction firm led by first-generation entrepreneur Mr. Vishnu Banwani, with over three decades of experience serving government and semi-government institutions. Our core work is the civil construction and renovation of public buildings — primary health centres, diagnostic facilities, railway stations and government offices — executed to the exacting standards public projects demand.

Every project is delivered turnkey under one roof: civil works, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, furniture and fit-outs, through to deep cleaning and final handover. With trained teams in sourcing, logistics and site execution — and long-standing relationships with the departments we serve — we turn approved designs into finished, functioning public spaces, on time and to specification.

Our services

Everything your project needs — under one roof.

Complete civil works for public buildings — plastering, waterproofing, tiling, flooring and structural renovation, executed to government specifications. We take on full project responsibility from site survey through to certified handover.

Renovation and upgradation of primary health centres, urban health centres and diagnostic facilities — including clinical-grade civil works, radiation-compliance construction, workstation fit-outs and medical equipment installation.

Civil, electrical, plumbing and finishing works at live, high-footfall railway stations — including projects under the Government of India's Amrit Bharat Station Scheme. Work is planned and executed to minimise disruption to active operations.

Construction and complete fit-out of offices, ministerial chambers and institutional spaces for government departments — delivered to premium institutional standards, on approved designs and within sanctioned budgets.

End-to-end wiring, switchgear and lighting installation across all project types — executed to approved electrical drawings and relevant safety standards, with full testing and certification on completion.

Comprehensive plumbing from pipework to sanitary fixtures, plus full installation of ventilation and air-conditioning systems — coordinated within the civil scope to avoid rework and ensure clean finishes.

Supply and installation of institutional, fixed and loose furniture, panelling and interior fit-outs — backed by three decades of sourcing relationships and delivered as part of our turnkey scope, eliminating the need for a separate vendor.

Paint touch-ups, snag resolution, deep cleaning of the entire site and structured final handover — including documentation, client walkthroughs and defect liability support. The project isn't done until the space is ready to use.
